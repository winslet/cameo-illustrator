# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about cutting vinyl — nothing here
is worth being unpleasant over.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to people whose expertise differs from yours. Many users here are
  crafters rather than programmers, and a question that seems basic to you took
  courage to ask.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the
  person.
- Assume good faith and mistakes over malice.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologise, and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/winslet). Reports will be handled discreetly, and
the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
